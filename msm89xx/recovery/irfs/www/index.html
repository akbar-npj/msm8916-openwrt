Sysupgrade (fresh install)

Upload a *-sysupgrade.bin. Writes kernel to boot, root to rootfs and erases the overlay (same as sysupgrade -n: no settings are kept).

  


  

    
Flash one partition

    
Upload a raw image and write it to the selected partition
      (e.g. *-squashfs-boot.img → boot,
      *-squashfs-system.img → rootfs).
