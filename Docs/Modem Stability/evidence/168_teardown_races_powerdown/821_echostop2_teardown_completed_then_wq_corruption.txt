predicts (a `/dev/kmsg` reader cannot be a liveness beacon).

## What this run proves

1. **Patch 821 does what it was written to do.** `flush_work()` in the
   `QCOM_SSR_BEFORE_SHUTDOWN` notifier returned (`T9 flush returned` at
   309.363296), `rproc_stop()` then proceeded to `q6v5_stop()`
   (`q6v5-trace 01..09`, 309.375474-309.375629) and the modem went down
   (`stopped remote processor`, 309.375823).  Compare the **earlier** 821 run,
   where the record ended on `T4` with no `T5` and no trace at all — i.e. the
   T4→T5 block is **intermittent**, not deterministic.
2. **A new failure follows immediately**: a wait-queue list corruption detected
   in `qmi-proxy`'s `poll()`.  It is a `WARNING` (not a panic), and the AP
   resets ~1 s later.
3. The T4→T5 timing in this run is **normal**: T4→T5 8.7 ms, T5→T6 11.8 ms,
   T6→T7 4.6 ms, T7→T8 13.8 ms, T8→T9 0.1 ms.  Nothing blocked.
