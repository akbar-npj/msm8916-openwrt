6. What is NOT in these logs, and why
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* `wwan0at0 disconnected` does not appear in any console-ramoops record above.
  That is EXPECTED and is not evidence: the line is `dev_info`
  (drivers/net/wwan/wwan_core.c:529) and is suppressed on every console at
  console_loglevel = 6. It can only reach the userspace-fed pmsg mirror.
  Use `q6v5-trace 01..09` (dev_err) as the marker of rproc_stop()'s progress.
* `stopped remote processor 4080000.remoteproc` is also `dev_info` and equally
  invisible, so it is not evidence either way.
* The pmsg-ramoops mirrors end at T3 rather than T4 in every case; pmsg is fed
  by a userspace `cat /dev/kmsg | while read` loop whose grep is block-buffered,
  so the last line it copied is not the last line the kernel stored.
