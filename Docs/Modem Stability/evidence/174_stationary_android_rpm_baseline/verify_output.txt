2. shutdown_req / wakeup_ind: free-running 19.2 MHz, and a WRAP TRAP
==============================================================================

A 19.2 MHz counter advances 2**32 ticks in 223.7 s, so ANY delta taken across a
window longer than ~224 s WRAPS and is meaningless.  Measured on the 10 s samples
(unambiguous: 192e6 ticks per interval, well under 2**32):

rpm_stationary.csv seg0: n=33 samples
  shutdown_req  observed/expected(19.2MHz*dt): p50=1.011 min=0.631 max=1.427
  wakeup_ind    observed/expected(19.2MHz*dt): p50=1.013 min=0.631 max=1.416
  long-span (WRAPPED, shown only to demonstrate the trap): shutdown_req delta over 324 s reads as 104.9 s of tick time => a bogus duty cycle of 32.4 %

rpm_stationary_boot2.csv seg0: n=178 samples
  shutdown_req  observed/expected(19.2MHz*dt): p50=0.985 min=0.000 max=4.230
  wakeup_ind    observed/expected(19.2MHz*dt): p50=0.982 min=0.000 max=9.581
  long-span (WRAPPED, shown only to demonstrate the trap): shutdown_req delta over 1792 s reads as 68.9 s of tick time => a bogus duty cycle of 3.8 %

=> ratio p50 = 1.00 for BOTH counters on BOTH boots: they are FREE-RUNNING, not
   gated.  An earlier draft of Doc 174 read the wrapped long-span delta as a
   "32.4 % duty cycle" and a "234 ms period" -- both were WRAP ARTEFACTS and are
   retracted here before publication.  The per-collapse wall period in section 1
   is derived from /proc/uptime, not from these counters, and is unaffected.
   The individual meaning of the two fields is NOT established: do not compute a
   modem duty cycle from them.

[self-check] all per-interval ratios in (0.5,1.6): True  -> free-running confirmed, wrap-trap confirmed
