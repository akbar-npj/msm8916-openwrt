This sample:  signature lte_ml1_sleepmgr_stm.c:4054,  900.862 s  ->  IN the short band.

=> n = 12 for the short band, and the band<->signature pairing holds again.  The
   short band is the one with the CLOCK signature (Doc 162: common_timer n=11 at

   same order as the other samples' spread.  It is one more independent
   confirmation that a modem-uptime clock, not an AP-side timer, gates this fatal.

not a controlled A/B.  It is offered as a consistency check on an existing finding,
not as new proof of it.
