=== WHY THE TWO PATHS DIFFER (mechanism, stated as a hypothesis, not a measurement) ===
Both paths end in the same qcom_smd_unregister_edge() -> qcom_smd_edge_release() ->
kfree(channel).  The difference is the CONTEXT that runs it:
  * `echo stop`: the sysfs write runs rproc_stop() in the writer's context, and with
    patch 821 the notifier's flush_work() waits for the teardown work, after which
    q6v5_stop() unregisters the edge.  The woken poller needs a context switch to
    reach poll_freewait(); on this path it does not get one -> deterministic UAF.
  * natural fatal: rproc_stop() is run from the recovery workqueue after the fatal is
    reported, and the modem's death wakes port->waitqueue (wwan_remove_port) with more
    scheduling opportunities in between, so the poller usually unlinks first.
This predicts the natural path is the SAME race at a much lower probability -- which
is exactly the ~1-in-21 figure Doc 159 measured -- and it is consistent with Doc 169
section 6 ("the 4 clean natural fatals remain open").  It is NOT proven here.
