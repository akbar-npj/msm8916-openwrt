6. WHY THE BEACON COULD NOT CHARACTERISE THE RESET -- AND THE FIX
-----------------------------------------------------------------
The old /overlay/beacon.sh began with:
    : > "$OUTDIR/beacon_a.txt"
    : > "$OUTDIR/beacon_b.txt"
i.e. it TRUNCATED both files at every boot, destroying the previous boot's tail --
the last advanced uptime before the reset, and the A-vs-B divergence that separates
"the FS/writeback path wedged" from "a global stall".  The one event it existed to
characterise was the one it could not.

FIX (deployed this round; source kept at scratch/beacon.sh):
    MARK="BOOT $(cut -d' ' -f1 /proc/uptime)s pid=$$ host=$(cat /proc/sys/kernel/hostname)"
    printf '%s\n' "$MARK" >> "$OUTDIR/beacon_a.txt"
    printf '%s\n' "$MARK" >> "$OUTDIR/beacon_b.txt"

Verified live after restarting the instance:
