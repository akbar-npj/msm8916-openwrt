THREE THINGS THIS PROVES, ALL FROM THE BINARY RATHER THAN THE SOURCE:
1. The drain sits between device_for_each_child() (the wake) and device_unregister()
   (the free) -- the ordering the fix depends on.
2. The bound is WALL CLOCK: `adrp x25, jiffies` + `add x21, x21, #0x258` and a
   `cmp x1, x21` loop test.  #0x258 = 600 = msecs_to_jiffies(2000) at HZ = 300, so
   the deadline is exactly 2.0 s and does NOT depend on msleep's granularity.
   (The rejected first revision -- `for (retries = 5000; retries--; ) msleep(1)` --
   had no jiffies reference at all, so this is a real difference, not a style one.)
3. It independently reproduces Doc 169 section 4's offset arithmetic from the
   COMPILER, not from pahole: &channel->fblockread_event = channel + 88 (0x58) and
   &...head = channel + 96 (0x60).  Those are the two constants the whole
   identification rests on.
