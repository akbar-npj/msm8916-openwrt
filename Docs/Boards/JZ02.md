# JZ02 (battery MiFi)

This page records the OpenWrt profile and hardware validation for the **JZ02**,
an MSM8916 battery MiFi with **three SIM switch lines** and one accessible
external slot. It was tested end-to-end on real hardware with both MTN and
Vodafone Ghana SIMs.

## Build identity and storage

- Build alias: `jz02`
- OpenWrt device profile: `generic-jz02`
- DTS: `msm8916-generic-jz02` (patch 868), based on `msm8916-ufi.dtsi`
- `SUPPORTED_DEVICES`: `jz02,generic`
- eMMC: 7,634,944 sectors × 512 bytes (4 GB)
- Recovery partition: **yes** (17-partition layout, `misc` + `recovery`) — see
  [Recovery](../RECOVERY/README.md)
- Ramoops: yes (`ramoops@8db00000`, 1 MiB)

## LEDs

Confirmed by toggling every GPIO on the board. The stock Android labels are
misleading — this mapping is the physical one.

| Lamp | GPIO | DTS name |
| :--- | ---: | :------- |
| network green | 11 | `green:wan` |
| network red | 31 | `red:wan` |
| network yellow | 9 | `yellow:wan` |
| Wi-Fi red | 6 | `red:wlan` |
| battery green | 8 | `green:bat` |
| battery red | 10 | `red:bat` |

GPIO 7 (hidden), 36, 117, 119 and **120** are unpopulated. `green:wlan` is
deliberately kept on GPIO120 as a no-op: `wifi-led-monitor` looks the node up by
name, and without it the monitor would fall back to `green:wan` and blink the
*network* lamp on Wi-Fi activity. The visible Wi-Fi lamp is **red-only**
(`red:wlan`, GPIO6).

## Battery

The pack is a **2100 mAh / 4.35 V** cell, not the stock Android
`palladium_1500mah` 4.2 V profile. The DTS uses:

- 3.35 V cutoff, 4.35 V charge limit, 2100 mAh
- 4.40 V design max (headroom for charger regulation, so health does not
  false-positive `OVERVOLTAGE`)
- a 31-point, 5-temperature OCV table scaled from the stock 4.2 V curve

Result on hardware: capacity 100 %, voltage 4.38 V, health `Good`.

> ⚠️ **The gauge is voltage-only.** `qcom,pm8916-bms-vm` has no current sense
> and no coulomb counter: `capacity` is a lookup of the loaded battery voltage
> in the OCV table. Under a heavy LTE/Wi-Fi load the voltage sags and the
> percentage can dip by a few points, then recover. Near the knee of the LiPo
> curve it can also sit on one value for a long time. This is a hardware
> property, not a board DTS bug. See [Battery & Charge Limit](../BATTERY.md).

## Reset / failsafe

**No software-visible reset button.** The stock DTB lists `key_f2` (GPIO37) and
`key_f3` (GPIO107), but neither responds to the physical pinhole. Hardware test
(2026-11-04):

- no transition on GPIO37, GPIO107 or any other TLMM GPIO while pressing it,
- no PMIC GPIO1–4 change,
- no `pm8941_pwrkey` event while the power button *does* emit `KEY_POWER`,
- no reboot.

The pinhole is therefore a hardware reset (or unpopulated), so `button_restart`
is **disabled** in the DTS rather than mapped to a guessed GPIO. Failsafe mode is
**not available** on this board — see [Failsafe](../Failsafe/README.md).

The separate **power button** is the PMIC `pm8941_pwrkey`: `pwrkey-monitor` gives
double-press reboot and long-press power-off, and a short press does nothing.

## SIM

The stock firmware exposes three switch lines plus a re-announce pulse, kept
verbatim as `gpio-leds` so userspace can drive them:

| Label | GPIO | Use |
| :---- | ---: | :-- |
| `sim1_switch_gpio` | 22 | embedded / solder SIM |
| `sim2_switch_gpio` | 23 | second SIM |
| `sim3_switch_gpio` | 1 | external slot |
| `sim_hotdet_gpio` | 20 | re-announce pulse |

`sim-select` (START=20) reads `sim.slot` from UCI (default **3**), pulses
`sim_hotdet`, asserts exactly one line, and rejects values outside 1/2/3. The
tested unit has only the **external slot 3** accessible; slots 1 and 2 are
internal/solder or unpopulated on this variant. The service exits immediately on
any other board (`/proc/device-tree/compatible` gate).

`sim-test` checks the selected slot, modem state, `wwan0` address, default
route, ping, DNS (through the resolver in `/etc/resolv.conf`) and HTTP.

## Modem

`qcom,a2-pin` on `&bam_dmux` is **required** for the `M8936FAAAANUZM`. Without
it the modem enters the `modem pc-ack timeout during resume` SSR loop once the
BAM channels idle.

Soak result: **11 h 45 min** modem-up on MTN LTE with a bearer, zero
`pc-ack timeout` events, `sim-test` 7/7. The JZ02 is deliberately kept out of
`/lib/himi-ok.sh` (different baseband); if a future unit does crash, the fix is
adding `*jz02*` to `modem-watchdog.recovery.himi_ok_boards`, not more a2-pin work.

## Boot race: `usb0` and `br-lan`

`network` starts at S20, but `/etc/init.d/usb-gadget` creates `usb0` at S99.
`br-lan` lists `usb0` as a port, so on the JZ02 netifd failed with `NO_DEVICE`
and the LAN never came up.

The fix is `network.@device[0].bridge_empty='1'` plus a net-hotplug rule that
attaches `usb0` when it appears. Both are **scoped to the JZ02** (`board_name` in
the uci-default, `/proc/device-tree/compatible` in the hotplug) so the existing
boards keep their bring-up unchanged.

## Validated on hardware

- MTN: `sim-test` 7/7, LTE, 96–100 % signal.
- Vodafone: `sim-test` 7/7, LTE, 70 % signal.
- Phone Wi-Fi: associates, gets a DHCP lease **with DNS**, internet works.
- Battery: 100 %, 4.38 V, health `Good`, no false over-voltage.
- LEDs: battery fast-blink gone when complete; red Wi-Fi off when healthy.
- AdGuard Home and Tailscale run on top of the image.
- CI: `build.yml -f device=jz02` completes and produces the full image set.

## Known limitations

- **Failsafe is unavailable** — there is no software-visible reset button.
- **Voltage-only fuel gauge** — percentage sags under load and is flat at the
  knee; see the battery note above.
- **Wi-Fi lamp is red-only**; the green Wi-Fi position is unpopulated.
- **Only the external SIM slot (3)** is accessible on the tested variant; the
  switch lines for slots 1/2 exist in hardware.

1. A discharge curve from a full pack (the voltage-only gauge benefits from a

2. Whether slots 1 or 2 are populated on your variant.
3. Whether your unit has a working reset pinhole (and if so, whether it is a

Open an issue with `dmesg`, `logread` and `mmcli -m 0`.
