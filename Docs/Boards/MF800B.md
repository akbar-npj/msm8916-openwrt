# MF800B

This page records the OpenWrt profile and hardware validation for the **MF800B**
MSM8916 modem stick. It is the project's first **battery** board (4.35 V cell)
and one of three boards with the 17-partition **recovery** layout.

## Build identity and storage

- Build alias: `mf800b`
- OpenWrt device profile: `generic-mf800b`
- DTS: `msm8916-generic-mf800b`
- `SUPPORTED_DEVICES`: `mf800b,generic`
- eMMC: 7,634,944 sectors × 512 bytes
- GPT total sectors: 7,634,944
- Recovery partition: **yes** (17-partition layout, `misc` + `recovery`) — see
  [Recovery](../RECOVERY/README.md)
- Ramoops: yes (`ramoops@8db00000`, 1 MiB)

## Battery

4.35 V / 2100 mAh pack with a PM8916 linear charger and voltage-mode fuel gauge.
Ships `luci-app-battery` (the **System → Battery** page, charge-limit monitor
and LED monitor). Charge is capped at **85 %** (resume 80 %) by default. See
[Battery & Charge Limit](../BATTERY.md).

## Reset / failsafe button

- GPIO **102**, **active-high, pull-down** (same wiring as HM-MIFIB-V12).
- Short press in first 7 s → failsafe; 5 s hold → factory reset.
- Verified on hardware? **Not yet** — declared active-high/pull-down but
  unconfirmed. Serial `f` key is the fallback.

## SIM

No `sim-presence` / `sim_hotplug` node is exposed in the MF800B profile, so
physical SIM hot-swap is **untested**. See [SIM Hot-Swap](../SIM_HOT_SWAP.md).

## Profile feature selection

Follows the common baseline:

- failsafe (button, unverified)
- Bluetooth
- WPA3-SAE and multi-SSID
- CPU CPR and the 800 MHz cap
- battery + charge limit (`luci-app-battery`)
- recovery partition

Deliberately **no A2 power-rail hold** (keeps normal runtime-suspend), and no
HMUF02-V5 thermal maps or `qcom-time-daemon`.

## Notes

The MF800B uses a **4.35 V** cell — do not copy the HM-MIFIB-V12 4.2 V battery
values into its device tree. See [Battery & Charge Limit](../BATTERY.md).
